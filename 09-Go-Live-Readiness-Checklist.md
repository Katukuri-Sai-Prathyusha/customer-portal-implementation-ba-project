# 09 - Go-Live Readiness Checklist

## 1. Purpose

The Go-Live Readiness Checklist is used to confirm that the customer portal is ready for production deployment.

The checklist ensures that business, technical, testing, security, training, support, and operational activities are completed before go-live approval.

## 9. Go-Live Decision Criteria

Go-live approval should be based on the following criteria:

- UAT is successfully completed.
- Business sign-off is received.
- Critical defects are resolved.
- High-priority defects are reviewed and accepted where appropriate.
- Required integrations are operational.
- Security approval is completed.
- Production environment is ready.
- Customer and support teams are prepared.
- Support processes are documented.
- Rollback procedures are available.
- Hypercare support is confirmed.

## 11. Go-Live Decision

### Decision: Pending

The project will proceed to production only after the required readiness criteria are completed and formal approval is obtained.

---

## 12. Hypercare

After production deployment, the project will enter a defined hypercare period.

During hypercare:

- Production issues will be closely monitored.
- Customer feedback will be reviewed.
- Critical incidents will be escalated quickly.
- Defects will be prioritized.
- Portal adoption will be monitored.
- Business stakeholders will receive regular updates.

After the hypercare period, ownership will transition to the normal support and operations teams.
