# 03 - Stakeholder Analysis & RACI Matrix

## 1. Stakeholder Analysis

Stakeholder analysis is used to identify individuals and teams affected by the customer portal implementation and determine their level of influence, interest, and involvement.

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Key Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Project Sponsor | High | High | Business value, budget, project progress |
| Project Manager | Project Management | High | High | Timeline, risks, dependencies, resources |
| Business Analyst | Requirements & Analysis | High | High | Requirements, stakeholder alignment, traceability |
| Product Manager | Product Owner | High | High | Product vision, priorities, customer value |
| Customer Support Manager | Business SME | High | High | Support efficiency and process improvements |
| Support Agents | End Users | High | Medium | Simple workflows and efficient case handling |
| Customers | External Users | High | High | Easy self-service experience |
| Development Team | Solution Delivery | High | Medium | Clear requirements and acceptance criteria |
| QA Team | Testing | High | Medium | Testable requirements and UAT support |
| IT Team | Infrastructure | Medium | Medium | System availability and integration |
| Security Team | Security & Compliance | Medium | High | Security and access controls |
| Training Team | Change Management | Medium | Medium | Training materials and user readiness |

## 2. Stakeholder Engagement Approach

### Manage Closely

Stakeholders with high interest and high influence:

- Executive Sponsor
- Project Manager
- Product Manager
- Business Analyst
- Customer Support Manager

Engagement approach:

- Regular project meetings
- Decision-making sessions
- Status reporting
- Risk and issue escalation
- Requirement sign-offs

### Keep Satisfied

Stakeholders with high influence but moderate interest:

- Security Team
- IT Leadership

Engagement approach:

- Periodic updates
- Approval checkpoints
- Risk and dependency discussions

### Keep Informed

Stakeholders with high interest but moderate influence:

- Support Agents
- QA Team
- Development Team
- Training Team

Engagement approach:

- Workshops
- Sprint discussions
- Requirement reviews
- UAT sessions
- Training sessions

### Monitor

Stakeholders with lower influence and lower involvement:

- Other internal departments

Engagement approach:

- Project announcements
- Periodic communication
- Updates when required

# 3. RACI Matrix

RACI represents:

- **R - Responsible:** Performs the activity.
- **A - Accountable:** Ultimately accountable for the outcome.
- **C - Consulted:** Provides input.
- **I - Informed:** Kept informed of progress or decisions.

| Activity | Sponsor | PM | BA | Product | Support | Dev | QA | IT | Security |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Initiation | A | R | C | C | I | I | I | I | I |
| Business Case | A | R | R | C | C | I | I | I | I |
| Requirement Gathering | I | C | R/A | C | C | C | C | C | C |
| Process Analysis | I | C | R/A | C | R | C | I | C | I |
| Scope Definition | A | R | R | C | C | C | I | C | I |
| User Stories | I | C | R/A | C | C | C | C | I | I |
| Solution Design | I | C | C | A | C | R | C | C | C |
| Development | I | A | C | C | I | R | C | C | I |
| System Testing | I | C | C | C | I | R | A/R | C | C |
| UAT Preparation | I | C | R/A | C | C | C | R | I | I |
| UAT Execution | I | C | A | C | R | C | R | I | I |
| Defect Management | I | A | R | C | C | R | R | C | I |
| Go-Live Readiness | A | R | R | C | C | C | C | R | C |
| Production Deployment | I | A | C | C | I | R | C | R | C |
| Project Closure | A | R | R | C | C | I | I | I | I |

# 4. BA Responsibilities

The Business Analyst plays a central role in connecting business stakeholders and the delivery team.

Key responsibilities include:

- Conducting stakeholder interviews
- Facilitating requirement workshops
- Documenting business requirements
- Analyzing current and future processes
- Creating user stories
- Defining acceptance criteria
- Managing requirement changes
- Supporting prioritization
- Maintaining requirement traceability
- Coordinating UAT
- Supporting defect clarification
- Communicating business impacts
- Supporting go-live readiness

## 5. Stakeholder Communication Plan

| Communication | Audience | Frequency | Owner | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Status Report | Sponsor / Leadership | Weekly | PM | Project progress |
| Requirement Workshop | Business / Technical Teams | As required | BA | Requirement clarification |
| Sprint Review | Project Team | Bi-weekly | Product | Demonstrate progress |
| Risk Review | PM / Project Team | Weekly | PM | Review risks and issues |
| UAT Meeting | Business / QA | During UAT | BA | Track UAT progress |
| Go-Live Meeting | All Key Stakeholders | Before Go-Live | PM | Readiness confirmation |

## 6. Stakeholder Management Principles

The project team will:

- Communicate decisions clearly.
- Document important stakeholder feedback.
- Resolve conflicting requirements through prioritization.
- Escalate unresolved decisions when required.
- Maintain clear ownership for project activities.
- Keep stakeholders informed about major changes.
