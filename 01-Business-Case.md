# 01 - Business Case

## 1. Business Background

The organization is a global company that provides products and services to customers across multiple regions.

Customers currently depend heavily on email and support teams for common activities such as submitting requests, checking request status, finding product information, and accessing support documentation.

As the customer base grows, the existing manual support process is becoming difficult to scale.

## 2. Current Business Challenges

The current process has several challenges:

- High volume of repetitive customer queries
- Heavy dependency on support agents
- Limited customer self-service
- Customers have limited visibility into request status
- Support information is distributed across different channels
- Manual tracking of customer requests
- Inconsistent customer experience
- Increased response time during periods of high support volume

## 3. Business Opportunity

Implementing a centralized customer self-service portal can provide customers with a single platform to access support services.

The portal can allow customers to:

- Search knowledge articles
- Submit support requests
- Track request status
- View previous requests
- Receive notifications
- Access relevant product information
- Communicate with support teams

This can reduce unnecessary manual interactions and allow support teams to focus on more complex customer issues.

## 4. Project Objectives

The primary objectives are:

1. Improve customer self-service capabilities.
2. Reduce repetitive support requests.
3. Improve visibility into customer requests.
4. Standardize the customer support process.
5. Improve support team productivity.
6. Provide a consistent customer experience.
7. Establish better reporting and visibility into support activities.

## 5. Expected Business Benefits

### Customer Benefits

- Faster access to information
- 24/7 access to self-service resources
- Better visibility into request status
- Easier submission and tracking of requests
- Improved customer experience

### Business Benefits

- Reduced support workload
- Improved operational efficiency
- Reduced repetitive manual activities
- Better support process visibility
- Improved scalability as customer volume increases

## 6. Key Success Measures

The project success can be measured using:

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Customer self-service adoption | Increase |
| Repetitive support requests | Reduce |
| Average response time | Reduce |
| Customer portal usage | Increase |
| Customer satisfaction | Improve |
| Support agent productivity | Improve |
| Portal adoption rate | Increase |

## 7. Key Assumptions

- Customers will have access to the portal.
- Existing customer information can be integrated with the portal.
- Support teams will participate in requirements validation and UAT.
- Knowledge base content will be available for migration.
- Required technical resources will be available.
- Training will be provided before go-live.

## 8. Key Constraints

- Limited project timeline
- Dependency on existing CRM/support systems
- Integration complexity
- Data migration requirements
- Customer adoption
- Availability of business and technical stakeholders

## 9. High-Level Risks

| Risk | Impact | Initial Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Integration delays | High | Identify dependencies early |
| Low customer adoption | High | Provide training and communication |
| Incomplete requirements | High | Conduct structured requirement workshops |
| Data migration issues | High | Perform validation and testing |
| UAT delays | Medium | Define UAT timeline and owners |
| Scope creep | High | Establish change control process |

## 10. Business Case Recommendation

The customer portal implementation is recommended because it can improve customer experience while reducing repetitive manual support activities.

The project should proceed with clearly defined scope, stakeholder ownership, project governance, risk management, and phased delivery.
